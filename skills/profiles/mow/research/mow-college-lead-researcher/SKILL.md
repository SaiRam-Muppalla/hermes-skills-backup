# Makers on Wheels: College Lead Researcher

Use this skill to research, score, and qualify engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

## Regional University Clusters
- **Telangana**: Osmania University (OU), JNTU Hyderabad (JNTU-H), Kakatiya University (KU).
- **Andhra Pradesh**: JNTU Kakinada (JNTU-K), JNTU Anantapur (JNTU-A), Andhra University (AU), SV University (SVU).

## Research Procedure
1. Search and identify target college website and placement cell.
2. Locate Training & Placement Officer (TPO) name, email, phone number, and LinkedIn.
3. Locate HODs for CSE, AI & Data Science, IT, and ECE.
4. Record NAAC accreditation grade (A++, A+, A, B) and Autonomous vs. Affiliated status.
5. Identify syllabus tech stack gaps (e.g. lack of hands-on Generative AI, Full-Stack, IoT, or Cloud deployment).
6. Format output as a markdown profile and save into Obsidian Vault under `College Intelligence/`.
